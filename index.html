FAQ

Questions sellers ask.

What are the steps to sell my car?

Fill out the form with your car's details — photos and the original window sticker help if you have them. A specialist reviews it and calls you with an offer. If you accept, we arrange the inspection and complete the paperwork together.

What information gives the most accurate offer?

The VIN, current mileage, options and packages, service history, any paint or bodywork, and clear photos of the exterior, interior and wheels.

Do you only buy exotics?

No. We buy Porsche, Mercedes-AMG, BMW M, G-Wagons and other luxury and performance cars, as well as exotics and collector cars.

Can I consign instead of selling outright?

Yes. We handle marketing, showing and negotiation, and your car is kept in our showroom with 24/7 security and climate control. Our commission is agreed up front and deducted at the sale.
